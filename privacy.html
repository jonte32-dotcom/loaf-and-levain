Privacy policy.

This is the privacy policy for loafandlevain.com, a free sourdough schedule calculator and resource for home bakers operated by Loaf & Levain ("we", "us", "our"). Last updated: 2026-05-09.

Plain-English summary

We don't sell your data. We don't track you across the internet. We collect the minimum needed to run a website (basic analytics) and an email list (your email if you choose to subscribe). You can leave any time, take your data with you, or ask us to delete it.

What we collect

If you visit the site:

  • Your IP address (logged briefly by Cloudflare for abuse prevention; deleted after 24 hours)
  • Your browser type and screen size (so the site renders correctly)
  • Anonymous page-view counts (so we know which articles get read)

If you subscribe to our newsletter:

  • Your email address (stored in MailerLite — see MailerLite's privacy policy)
  • Whether you opened or clicked our emails (used to improve our content; you can opt out by unsubscribing)

If you buy our Pro PDF:

  • Your name, email, and billing details — handled by Gumroad (see Gumroad's privacy policy); we never see your full payment card
  • Your purchase record (so we can send you lifetime updates)

If you click our gear recommendations:

  • Amazon receives information about your visit to their site (see Amazon's privacy notice)
  • We earn a small commission if you purchase through our link, at no extra cost to you

If you save your bake plan locally, we use your browser's localStorage. That data stays on your device and never leaves it. Clear your browser cache and it's gone.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone
  • We don't use third-party advertising trackers beyond Google AdSense (only after explicit consent via our cookie banner — see below)
  • We don't profile you or build behavioural models about you
  • We don't send unsolicited marketing email; you only get our newsletter if you sign up for it

Cookies and similar technologies

We use minimal cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for the site to function (saving your bake schedule, remembering your unit preference). No consent needed under GDPR.
  • Analytics cookies — anonymous, aggregate usage data only.
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google AdSense if you give explicit consent via our consent banner. EU/EEA visitors see a consent prompt before any ad cookies are placed.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — ask us what data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — ask us to delete your data
  • Right to data portability — get your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to specific processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint — with your national data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@loafandlevain.com. We respond within 30 days.

Children's privacy

The site is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in some EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Data retention

  • Newsletter subscribers: data kept until you unsubscribe, then anonymised within 30 days
  • Pro PDF customers: data kept for 7 years (tax/accounting requirements)
  • Server logs: 24 hours
  • Anonymous analytics: 14 months

Where your data is stored

  • Server logs and site files: Cloudflare (global edge)
  • Newsletter subscribers: MailerLite (EU servers)
  • Pro PDF transactions: Gumroad (US servers, GDPR-compliant)
  • Backups: Cloudflare Pages internal storage

For EU/EEA users: international data transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where applicable.

Third-party services we use

  • Cloudflare — hosting, CDN, security
  • MailerLite — newsletter delivery
  • Gumroad — Pro PDF sales
  • Amazon Associates — gear recommendations (affiliate links)
  • Google AdSense — display advertising (with your consent)
  • Pinterest — content distribution; we publish pins linking to articles
  • GitHub — code repository

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be announced via the newsletter to existing subscribers.

Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints:
