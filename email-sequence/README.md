# Email welcome sequence

5 emails to drop into ConvertKit (or Mailchimp/Substack/whatever you use).
Set them up as an automation triggered by the cheat sheet form submission.

## Schedule

| # | When | Subject | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Immediate | Your sourdough cheat sheet (+ a heads-up) | Deliver PDF, set expectations |
| 02 | Day 2 | The thing that ruins most home loaves | Build authority, give value |
| 03 | Day 5 | A quick story about a $19 mistake | Soft Pro intro via narrative |
| 04 | Day 9 | What's actually inside Sourdough Schedule Pro | Make the offer concrete |
| 05 | Day 14 | Closing the launch window (last chance) | Direct CTA with light scarcity |

## Tone notes

- Write from "I" not "we" — feels like a person, not a brand
- Keep emails short (200–400 words)
- One link per email, max two
- No tracking pixels in the body (people see them and unsubscribe)
- Plain text or minimal HTML — fancier emails get filtered to Promotions tab

## Setup in ConvertKit

1. Create a Sequence named "Sourdough Welcome"
2. Trigger: subscribed to "Cheat Sheet" form
3. Email 1: send immediately, attach cheat-sheet.pdf
4. Email 2: schedule 2 days after subscribe
5. Email 3: 5 days after
6. Email 4: 9 days after
7. Email 5: 14 days after
8. Tag "completed-welcome" after email 5 — exclude from future sequences targeting cold subs

After the welcome series, drop them into your weekly newsletter cadence.
