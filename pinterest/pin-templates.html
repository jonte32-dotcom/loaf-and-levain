Pin 3 — Gummy crumb diagnosis

03 Troubleshooting

Why is my sourdough gummy?

  • 1. You cut it warmWait 1+ hour after bake
  • 2. Internal temp too lowAim for 96–99°C
  • 3. Underproofed bulkExtend 1–2 h or raise inoc
  • 4. Overproofed bulkShorten or drop inoc 5%
  • 5. Not enough steamUse Dutch oven covered 20 min

Pin 4 — DDT formula

04 Pro baker secret

Get the perfect dough temp.

Water = (Target × 4)
− Flour − Room − Friction
TARGET Aim 24–26°C
FLOUR Usually = room temp
ROOM Measure, not the thermostat
FRICTION 2–4°C hand · 5–8°C mixer

Solve for water. Use a thermometer to verify.

Pin 5 — Starter readiness checklist

05 Starter check

Is your starter ready?

4 of 6 = ready to mix. Skip the float test, watch the rise.

  • At least doubled since feeding
  • Domed top, not flat
  • Bubbles visible on the sides
  • Smells like beer + yogurt
  • Stretchy gel consistency
  • Floats in water (optional)
