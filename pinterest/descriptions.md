## Posting strategy

**Frequency:** 2–3 pins per week. Pinterest favours consistency over volume. Five pins on Tuesday is worse than one pin every other day.

**Best times:** Saturday afternoon, Sunday morning. Sourdough searchers are mostly weekend bakers.

**Boards to create:**
- Sourdough Tutorials (everything how-to)
- Sourdough Troubleshooting (gummy, dense, flat — every fix)
- Sourdough Recipes (focaccia, ciabatta, etc.)
- Sourdough Tools & Tips (calculator, cheat sheet, gear)
- Bread Photography (visual pins for engagement)

**Pin-to-pin variation:** rotate visual styles across posts. Pinterest's algorithm penalises sameness. The 10 templates above span dark/light/colour-block/photo-style intentionally so 6 weeks of pinning don't all look alike.

**The first 30 days:** post manually. Learn what gets impressions vs saves. After 30 days, batch a month's worth in Buffer or Tailwind and let them auto-post.
