## Hydration → Flour & Water (per 500 g flour)

| Hydration | Water | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| 65% | 325 g | Sandwich, beginner |
| 70% | 350 g | Country, easy |
| 75% | 375 g | Country, classic |
| 78% | 390 g | Open crumb push |
| 80% | 400 g | Focaccia |
| 85% | 425 g | Ciabatta, advanced |
| 100% | 500 g | Pan de cristal |

**Whole-grain rule:** add 5–8% to whatever the recipe says.

## Bulk fermentation time at 20% inoculation

| Room temp | Bulk duration |
|---|---|
| 16°C / 61°F | ~11 h |
| 18°C / 64°F | ~9 h |
| 20°C / 68°F | ~7.5 h |
| 22°C / 72°F | ~6.2 h |
| 24°C / 75°F | ~5 h |
| 26°C / 79°F | ~4 h |
| 28°C / 82°F | ~3.3 h |
| 30°C / 86°F | ~2.7 h |

**Q10 rule:** rate doubles every 8°C rise. Halve inoculation → roughly double the time.

## Starter readiness — 6-point check

- [ ] At least doubled since feeding
- [ ] Domed top
- [ ] Bubbles on the sides
- [ ] Smells like beer and yogurt
- [ ] Stretchy, gel-like consistency
- [ ] (Optional) floats in water

4 of 6 = ready to mix.

---

## DDT (Desired Dough Temperature) formula

`Water temp = (Target × 3) − Flour temp − Room temp − Friction`

- **Target:** 24–26°C is the sweet spot
- **Flour temp:** measure (often = room temp)
- **Room temp:** measure (not the thermostat)
- **Friction:** 2–4°C for hand mix, 5–8°C for stand mixer

---

## Bake schedule (Dutch oven, 250°C / 480°F)

1. Preheat empty Dutch oven 60 min
2. Score, transfer dough, lid on, bake 20 min
3. Lid off, drop to 230°C / 450°F, bake 22–28 min
4. Internal temp 96–99°C / 205–210°F = done
5. Cool on rack 60 min minimum before slicing

---

## Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | Most likely cause |
|---|---|
| Gummy crumb | Underbaked, undercooled, OR underproofed |
| Dense crumb | Underproofed bulk (most common), weak starter |
| Flat loaf | Overproofed bulk, OR over-retarded |
| No oven spring | Underproofed OR cold Dutch oven |
| Pale crust | Oven not hot enough, no steam, or short bake |
| Crumb wall holes | Tight final shape OR overproofed |

---

## Stretch & fold timing

- Set 1: 30 min after bulk start
- Set 2: 60 min
- Set 3: 90 min  
- Set 4: 120 min
- Stop folds when dough is smooth, holds shape, jiggles

---

## Salt percentage cheat

- 1.8% — slightly under-salted, faster ferment
- 2.0% — standard
- 2.2% — slightly over-salted, slower ferment, better keeping
