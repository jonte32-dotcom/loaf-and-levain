Starter readiness need 4 of 6

  • At least doubled since feeding
  • Domed top (not flat or sunken)
  • Bubbles visible on sides
  • Smells like beer + yogurt
  • Stretchy, gel-like consistency
  • Floats in water (optional)

Desired Dough Temperature (DDT)

Water = (Target × 4) − Flour − Room − Friction
Target 24–26°C ideal
Friction 2–4°C hand
Flour = room temp
Friction 5–8°C mixer

Bake schedule (Dutch oven 250°C)

  1. Preheat empty Dutch oven 60 min
  2. Score, transfer, lid on, bake 20 min @ 250°C
  3. Lid off, drop to 230°C, bake 22–28 min
  4. Internal temp 96–99°C = done
  5. Cool on rack 60 min before slicing
